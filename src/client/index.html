        Continue?

        Explore at your pace.

        Explore at ease. Explore is your starting tool. Drag to pan and tap an existing marker to open its note.

        Brush across tiles. Choose the brush, then Reveal or Refog. Tap a tile or drag with one finger or pointer to paint a path. Refog restores the original map. One Undo reverses the whole stroke.

        Move naturally. Use two fingers to move and pinch to zoom while brushing. With a mouse, hold Space or the middle button to pan. Scroll to zoom. The fit button shows the whole map, and the journal finds a tile by coordinate.

        Leave a note. Choose Marker and tap any tile. Tap a marker in Explore or a journal entry to edit its note.

        Keep a game log. Choose Log and tap a tile to record a trip day and its notes. Open the book button to browse days, write without a location, or place a day on the map. The journey button shows or hides your recorded route.

        A shared journey. The map and game log update automatically. Sign in with an editor passphrase to make changes. All saved changes are visible to everyone.

        Go full screen. In Safari, choose Share → Add to Home Screen and enable Open as Web App if shown. Open the new SeerJourney icon. Use an HTTPS address for offline availability.

        Make room for adventure.

        On iPhone or iPad, open this atlas in Safari, then:

        1. Tap Share .
        2. Choose Add to Home Screen.
        3. Keep Open as Web App enabled if shown, then tap Add.

        Launch the SeerJourney icon for an edge-to-edge map. Connect to the internet to see the latest campaign and save edits.

        Home-screen installation needs a web address. Campaign updates require an internet connection. Save session downloads a portable backup.
